This directory contains the following four examples of source material used to produce the Canadian Notifiable Disease Incidence Dataset (CANDID).

* `cdi_ca_1965_wk_prov_mcgill_p1-page-15.pdf` is from the "Notifiable Diseases -- Weekly Summary" for the week ending January 23, 1965, from Statistics Canada.
* `cdi_mort_on_1939-1969_wk-page-2.pdf` is a handwritten spreadsheet of disease incidence in Ontario in 1939, from the Ontario Ministry of Health.
* `cdi_mumps_ca_1924-55_wk_prov-page-13.pdf` is a handwritten spreadsheet of mumps incidence in 1936, obtained from Statistics Canada.
* `cdi_sk_1910-1927_mn-page-4.pdf` is from the "Annual Report of the Bureau of Public Health for the Province of Saskatchewan (1910)".
